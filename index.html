Our Journey & Mission

Ingenuity Over Resources: The Spirit of Team Galaxy

In Ikom, Cross River State, advanced robotics labs do not come pre-packaged. When our students set out to build, they started with what was in their hands: cardboard boxes, copper wire, recycled plastic, and DC toy motors. They sketched schematics on poster paper and tested autonomous code on borrowed laptops.

That relentlessly resourceful spirit took them from a school workshop in Ikom all the way to the National Robotics Championship stage in front of the Federal Ministry of Education, Coderina, and top multinational corporate sponsors.

Today, Team Galaxy is not only competing with metal FTC chassis and advanced autonomous control loops — they are proving to every youth in Cross River State that world-class engineering can come from anywhere.
