FTC Nigeria National Design Award Winners • Team 21131

From Cardboard Prototypes to National Champions.

In 2023, high school students at Federal Government College, Ikom built their first autonomous mover using scrap cardboard, hot glue, and toy motors. Twelve months later, they defeated hundreds of competitor schools in Abuja to win the FTC Nigeria Championship Design Award.

Now, their aging equipment has reached its limit. We are raising ₦3,750,000 (~$2,500 USD) to replace worn motors, controllers, and battery packs for the 2025/2026 competition season.

Technical Dossier • Case Study

Why We Won the National Design Award

Many high school teams in metropolitan commercial centers begin with imported aluminum build kits and corporate-funded robotics labs. Team Galaxy did not.

Our journey began inside an ordinary classroom in Ikom. Before the students ever touched an extrusion channel, they solved the geometry on graph paper. They built functional prototypes out of discarded packaging cardboard to verify drive ratios, wheel clearance, and weight balance.

When our robot arrived at the National FTC Championship inspection table, the judges recognized what made it extraordinary: every millimeter was intentional. The intake mechanism wasn't copied from an internet tutorial — it was engineered through twenty iterations of physical testing.

That is why the judges awarded Team Galaxy the National Design Award. Sponsoring this team is an investment in genuine African problem solvers who do not wait for ideal conditions to build world-class things.
